## A. Box construction

- **R1.** Box = consolidation bounded by sellers (top) and buyers (bottom): "identify where buyers or sellers excuse me control the price" (L19–21); buyers' "job is to Simply hold that price up" (L27–29). c=0.95
- **R2a (V1).** Edges are closes, wicks ignored: "regardless of where the wick goes it's where the candle closes that's ultimately where sellers control the price" (L129–133). c=0.85 as V1's rule.
- **R2b (V5/V8/V9, disagrees).** Edges are "points of contact", clustered wicks count: "two taps, same wicks, that's the floor or ceiling" (L3792–3793); "whether it's a candle close or wicks it does not matter" (L6529–6533); "just wicks or just bodies ... every chart needs your discretion" (L6639–6641). Reconciliation: one timeframe down "those wicks have turned into bodies" (L6547–6551). Net: closes preferred, repeated wicks accepted. c=0.8
- **R3.** Touches: "the magic number three ... three off the top three off the bottom" (L87–89); "three points of contact, I would say, for the mo most part" (L3626); later "two to three rejections, candle closes on the bottom and the top in order to be defined as a potter box" (L6431–6437); "it doesn't always have three" (L3992). A rejection is a wave: "two candles does not count as two rejections" (L7171–7173). One-up/one-down "does not qualify for a box for me. It's a range" (L3601). Third wave usually breaks (L6621–6627). c=0.85
- **R4.** Duration (per-ticker averages, not limits): Tesla "if it goes past 8 to 9 days, it's going to consolidate for a whole month" (L5060–5063); SPY "about 7 to 10 days of solid sideways price action ... leads to expansion" (L7441–7445); trending pullbacks "4 to 5 days" (L7609); "definitely not going to do it after its second day in range" (L7461–7463). No minimum bar count beyond the waves. c=0.8
- **R5.** Fractal, top-down: "if you can find it on the weekly then there should be the same things down on the bottom right with more detail" (L13–17); a 24h range may be a box on the 4h (L6481–6487); "Lower time frame, lower return" (L2759). c=0.95
- **R6.** "overlapping boxes this is actually a good thing" (L57–59); a box centred on another's floor gives the "snowball Chain Reaction" (L745–751). c=0.85
- **R7.** Lookback: "looking left to a degree but I'm not going to five or six years ago" (L187–189); targets may still come from 1999 (L4330–4333). c=0.7
- **R8.** Range ≠ box: a range gives no cost-basis signal, only "if you get the 50% mark, you'll get the top of that range" (L6363–6373). c=0.9
- **R9.** A punched-through box is "null and void"; its top and midline remain bounce levels only (L3946–3963). c=0.85
- **R10.** Quality: clear top/bottom with many closes at one level is high quality; "ton of wicks and too much going on" is low (L3885–3899); "small box low volume is not really worth my time" (L763–765); some charts are "unboxable" (L3704). Box drawn with a 50% line and dragged forward (L119, L1069–1075). c=0.8
- **R11 (images).** IMG1: "Intel Corporation, 4h". Gray box ≈$19.0–$20.70 from ≈Jul 29, extended to the right edge; dotted midline ≈$19.85 (DS L18 says "$20.00" and "$19.00 and $21.00" — both slightly off). Labels 21.43, 20.70 (top), 18.89 (just under bottom), 17.84; a second box ≈$13.0–14.6 (yellow midline ≈13.75) far below; nothing drawn between 17.84 and 14.6 (the empty space). Three cyan circles mark three top rejections (≈Aug 4, 6, 11): each wick pierces 20.70, each body closes inside; lows ≈Aug 7–8 wick to 18.89 while bodies hold ≈19.0. So edges sit at body extremes with wicks outside; three top touches, ≈2–3 bottom; box forms right after the "E" earnings collapse. IMG2 is the SAME INTC drawing, not QQQ daily — DS Example 2 (L21–27) has no image. c=0.75

## B. Cost basis

- **R12.** The 50% line: "usually in most it is always found at the 50 Mark of any area of consolidation" (L99–101); "cost basis is the 50% mark of any area of consolidation" (L5771); from volume profile, "the majority of the volume at the 50% mark" (L5723–5725). c=0.95
- **R13.** "cost spaces is king" (L35); "It's the most meaningful for me ... what says you're going up or down" (L3643–3644). c=0.95
- **R14.** "break over your cost basis you will go to resistance ... close below that cost basis you will go to support" (L83–87); "if candles opened up above that, they ripped. If candles went up to them, they rejected" (L5725–5727); "candle close above or open above is typically 9.8 times out of 10 going to give you a new high" (L5738–5741). c=0.9
- **R15.** The open matters: "it is always the open" (L549–553); "the only candle that opens on top of cost basis therefore giving you a new high" (L373–377). c=0.75
- **R16.** Also a buy/add level: "I definitely don't mind adding to positions at cost basis" (L4200–4203). c=0.85

## C. Timeframes

- **R17.** 24-hour extended-hours candles, never RTH daily: "I do not use the daily ... I only use the 24hour setup this is imperative" (L1027–1033); "I use them on the 24hour time frame only" (L1985); "don't bother if you're not turning on your extended hours data" (L6899–6903); "I can't even put out regular market hours because I hardly care" (L3919); DS L9 agrees. Session bounds once, garbled: "shows price action from 4:30 to 9" (L5699). c=0.98
- **R18.** "24-hour time frame and weekly is where my trades come from" (L3439–3443); swing entries "Typically the 24hour candle close and the 4 hour" (L3779–3780); day trades "the 4 hour, 1 hour, 15 minute, and then whatever three or five" (L3686–3687). Weekly close strongest (L2525–2527). c=0.9
- **R19.** Pre/post-market candles are read: "switch it on and off in order to to figure out where those candles actually open" (L383–385); "the majority of the movement in the market happens in after hours" (L855–857); pre-market structure counts (L735–737); "80% of the market movement is overnight" (L2205). c=0.9

## D. Entry triggers

- **R20.** Master trigger: "when a candle closes on the 24-hour time frame outside of structure or punching through a 50% mark of a box" (L2271–2275); intraday breaks are provisional: "we can't say that price has been broken on cost basis until the end of the day" (L6941–6943). c=0.95
- **R21.** Click time: "I buy at the end of the day" (L2975); "350 rolls around at 350, 355"; ETFs "till about 4:15. I'll buy right before the market's closed" (L2979–2983), so "the highest time frame candle ... is giving me the signal" (L2983–2985). c=0.9
- **R22.** Cost-basis RECLAIM (punch back) is the primary entry: "we've broken that cost basis at 181 and so that promises us a new high" (L253–255); "when cost basis is broken, that's a punch back, right? That guarantees me my new high" (L2289–2291); "reclaims its floor, breaks cost basis, and that's what promises you a new high" (L3649–3651); punch backs are "the entirety of everything that I do" (L91–93). c=0.95
- **R23.** Floor reclaim / close-against-the-wall is an earlier, smaller entry: "by losing the floor then reclaiming it you are now bullish up to cost basis" (L247–251); "closes green flat up against where sellers pulled the price down. That was a no-brainer" (L2119–2121); "closes outside of the structure or closes up against it" (L2977); "A lot of the times I take it as it leaves" (L5312–5313). c=0.85
- **R24.** Retest after a gap-up: "even if you miss taking it here, you can take it there" (L6727–6731); a gap over the level is itself confirmation (L5908–5909). c=0.8
- **R25.** Lower-TF triggers: "hourly candle close or break outside of that wall" (L5436–5439); "a 15-minute candle above 364 or ... a 30 minute candle above that previous mark" (L3593–3594). c=0.85
- **R26.** Pre-emptive floor buys: "if there's empty space above, 100% ... I'll take that preemptively" (L3794–3796). c=0.8
- **R27.** Bearish mirror: "this goes for the up or the down" (L209–211); "Punch down would be just 24hour candle close through or uh closed underneath that cost basis. and that would trigger a new low" (L5779–5780); targets "counts for red, too" (L4318–4323). c=0.9

## E. The punch back

- **R28.** A box "loses its floor, right? Goes back, retests something underneath it" then "reclaims it and punches or moves through the 50% mark" (L4126–4130; L209–223). c=0.95
- **R29.** Chain reaction: "it sets off a chain reaction" (L259); "you break cost basis speed it up right break another cost basis speed it up" (L829–833); the promised high is the next box's cost basis (L255–257). c=0.9
- **R30.** Fast fall precedes it: "The way you rip is the way you fall" (L2677); "If you dump outside of a box and you do it fast, when that floor is recovered, you're going to rip up" (L4066–4068). c=0.85
- **R31.** Failure: re-entering a box from above → "we make a new low" (L459–461); a punch back can be "invalidated" (L4492–4495; mechanism UNSPECIFIED); "Rejections come after price has lost something and did not recover it" (L7141–7143); low-liquidity names may not punch back (L5670–5685). c=0.75

## F. Empty space

- **R32.** "empty space or solid candles with no structure in them" (L2533–2535); "the space in between two boxes" (L4859–4862); "created by large moves either up or down" (L6649); not an ICT fair-value gap (L5531–5535). c=0.95
- **R33.** "if you have empty space, you will fill it quickly" (L4870–4873); "Quickly. Both ways" (L7043); "we are trading boxto box, point A to point B" (L4841–4843). c=0.9
- **R34.** Target = first structure left: "it is always the first green candle to your left" (L4296–4299), its bottom / the first consolidation (L4444–4451); downside targets are red candles / "The top of the previous box" (L4318–4323, L3838–3839). c=0.85
- **R35.** 50% trim: "the only place that I would be trimming or taking profit is at the 50% mark within that empty space" (L5933–5935); the midpoint of an empty-space candle is an "imbalance" (L2731–2737); "come back for supplies after you're halfway" (L6809–6811); yet an imbalance "should not be what stops you" (L4932–4935). c=0.85
- **R36.** "Empty space or gaps? Empty space" (L4164–4165); "95 percent of gaps are always going to get filled" (L571); no empty space → longer expiry or skip (L2577–2583, L1755–1757). "Meaningful trigger" exists only in DS L26; in T a trigger is the level whose close/break starts the move (L1595–1597, L4866–4869). c=0.6

## G. Exits and management

- **R37.** "we do trade from point A to point B. Leave some runners" (L2959–2961); "holding from point A to point B and not giving a [ __ ] in between" (L5937–5939). c=0.95
- **R38.** Trim at first target: "when our first target is hit ... we're taking the original investment off" (L2939–2941); "I said once it hits cost basis I'm out" (L2659–2663); "once my target is hit, I expect consolidation again" (L5357–5359). c=0.85
- **R39.** Stop: "When a stock closes back in the box, is that what you use to trigger your stop-loss? Yes" (L3689); "on the nearest structure underneath me" (L2123–2125); "any floor cost basis or ceiling of a box if you can set a stop-loss on it" (L4242–4245). Overnight "there really is none ... hedge" (L3565–3569). c=0.9
- **R40.** Hold time: sell "in the morning around 10:30 11:00" (L2183–2185); "three consecutive days ... the average time that I'm holding swing trades" (L7151–7155); "stop trading them for more than 2 to 3 days" (L7929–7933); after "3 to 4 days of movement in one direction ... you're going to stop" (L7547–7553). No fixed bar count. c=0.85
- **R41.** No DCA except off structure (L2081–2083, L4775–4777); roll "each time a cost basis was broken" (L609–611); "I monitor it the next day but not after hours" (L3802–3803). c=0.75

## H. Options specifics

- **R42.** "I don't trade shares, B, uh I trade options" (L2089–2091); equity only where options are unavailable (L2097–2113). c=0.9
- **R43.** "I trade out of the money" (L4856); "I take target-based contracts" (L3313) — strike at the empty-space target, bought "far out of the money" while consolidation cheapens them (L3969–3973). c=0.95
- **R44.** "we buy them for 10 cents to 25 cents" (L2189); "$10, $15, usually I don't go higher than 50" (L2991–2993); "$2 to $3 ... $5 to $6" (L5094–5096). c=0.9
- **R45.** DTE: "I buy the week of contracts when I'm moving through empty space" (L2577–2579); "a week extra" per resistance (L2583; "Per 24-hour resistance ... a week of expiration on top" L5995–5997); "a week or two" (L6929); no empty space → "outside of that 25day framework" (L7069–7071); "Do you always go out two weeks? No" (L4180–4182); next-day ETF expiry "you're cooked" if the target misses (L3563–3565); breakdowns need longer expiry, "you can expect a retest" (L7937–7947). c=0.8
- **R46.** "Your contract's value is not important. It is not the reason that you got into the trade" (L5927–5928) — aimed at traders who exit because "I was up 130% or two 200%" (L5926). c=0.95
- **R47.** Greeks: "I'm not super concerned with it ... I'm more focused on the actual strike" (L4914–4920); max pain inside boxes makes contracts cheap (L3371–3389, L5085–5089); "longer contracts inherently have less decay" (L5807–5809); earnings "IV can start stretching" (L6785–6787). c=0.85
- **R48.** Sizing: "$300 to $400 worth" on "a $50,000 account" (L5096–5100); small account "minimum 50 bucks" of $300 (L5537–5549). Hedge = cheap opposite-side contracts (L3569–3571, L7077–7081). c=0.8
- **R49.** Earnings: "I do trade earnings. I do not trade them on a regular basis and I do have a very strict criteria" (L6667–6669); both sides "almost like a long strangle" (L6753–6755, L7247–7259). c=0.85

## I. Daily workflow / cadence

- **R50.** Lives "8:30 in the morning ... 300 p.m. Eastern time every day. Sunday live at 6:30" (L4830–4833); charting "24 hour end of day ... 4 hour" (L5460–5463). c=0.9
- **R51.** Morning: "every morning you should probably know where you're at" (L349); "if you open a 24-hour candle up inside of itself nine times out of 10 you are going to consolidate" (L5017–5019) → "we're inside day. I'm not I'm not dealing with this" (L5022); plan on "the lower time frame setup ... on the hourly" (L2741–2749). c=0.85
- **R52.** Watchlist: post-its and alerts (L2301–2311, L2441); heat-map, chart one by one (L4755–4759); "build a watch list of things that are yet to break" (L7881–7883). c=0.85
- **R53.** Frequency: "on a busy week five or six times. on a choppy week, maybe two or three times ... weeks where I've taken one trade" (L4166–4176); "You could take a trade once a week" (L5077–5079); ETFs "two weeks ... then I'll I'll not touch any ETFs for another two weeks" (L5613–5616); "I hit them and I quit them" (L7265–7267). "Smashes trades every day" is not in the sources; he day-trades only "when the same opportunities that I would take for a swing trade present themselves" (L7195–7201). c=0.9
- **R54.** In range → nothing: "when I do not have a signal ... I tend to fade out" (L1725–1727); "I don't trade in chop. I buy when the chop is over" (L5065–5067). c=0.9

## K. Filters / avoid list

- **R55.** All-time highs: "my strategy and what I teach is not useful ... I am not an all-time highs trader" (L1891–1895). c=0.9
- **R56.** Inside boxes/ranges: "I don't trade in boxes because boxes are what make contracts so cheap through max pain" (L3371–3373); "that is where losses occur" (L5011–5013); weekly range "a nightmare to trade" (L7367–7371); no empty space = "torture chamber" (L1869–1873). c=0.9
- **R57.** Liquidity: "stocks that have daily trading volume ... not ... penny stocks or biofarmaceuticals" (L7331–7339); illiquid options "typically I stay away" (L4848–4854); "If it has volume, it will run" (L3976). c=0.9
- **R58.** News: "the chart was already set up to dump so I don't want to hear the news did it" (L887) — not a filter; an overrun consolidation implies news → strangle (L7255–7259). c=0.75
- **R59.** "None. Zero. No indicators, no flow, no nothing" (L5531–5533); no patterns/trendlines (L475–477); channels only as "confluences" (L6169–6170). c=0.9
- **R60.** Opening inside yesterday's range → chop (L5017–5025, L7823–7831); prefer names "coming or currently in areas of consolidation" (L5315–5317). c=0.85

## L. Contradictions and ambiguities

1. Wicks: "it's where the candle closes" (L131) vs "two taps, same wicks, that's the floor" (L3792) and "candle close or wicks it does not matter" (L6531); IMG1 wicks sit outside both edges.
2. Touches: "magic number three" (L87) vs "two to three rejections" (L6433) vs "doesn't always have three" (L3992).
3. Timeframe: "I only use the 24hour setup" (L1029–1031) vs 4h/1h/30m/15m entry closes (L3779, L3593, L5436). 24h ETH is the chart type; signals are multi-TF.
4. Certainty: "it's a guaranteed new high" (L5740) vs "it either works or it doesn't" (L3855) and "the few times that it hasn't I completely disregard" (L3973–3975).
5. Timing: "I buy at the end of the day" (L2975) vs "I take it as it leaves" (L5313) and pre-emptive floor buys (L3795).
6. Exit: "point A to point B" (L2959) vs "stop trading them for more than 2 to 3 days" (L7931–7933) and "hit them and I quit them" (L7267).
7. Day trading "is [ __ ]" (L2201–2207) vs "I day trade when the same opportunities ... present themselves" (L7195–7201).
8. Range gives "nothing other than the resistance up above" (L6801–6803) yet Celsius/PLTR ranges were traded (L6999–7005, L6179–6181).
9. Cost basis "usually ... always found at the 50 Mark" (L99–101) vs flatly "is the 50% mark" (L5771).
10. Empty space ≠ FVG (L5531–5535) but "Every time you fill a gap, you're going to bounce" (L2929–2933).
11. Session bounds "4:30 to 9" (L5699): AM/PM unstated.
12. DS vs images: DS L18 levels differ from IMG1 (≈19.0–20.7, midline ≈19.85); IMG2 is mislabeled (INTC 4h, not QQQ daily).
